# Mimi 耳 privacy policy

_Last updated: 28 November 2026_

Mimi is a free app for learning Japanese. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. This page explains
what happens to your information when you use it.

## What Mimi stores

Everything you create in Mimi stays on your phone: your level and goals from onboarding, saved words, review
schedule, quiz results, streaks, badges, settings and your reminder time. It is kept in the app's private storage and
is never sent to us. We don't run any servers and can't see it.

Deleting the app, or using **Profile > Danger zone > Delete everything**, removes all of it. A backup you export from
**Profile > Back up my data** is plain text that you choose where to keep or share.

## What Mimi sends to other services

Some features need the internet. When you use them, your phone talks directly to these services, which see the same
things any website sees (such as your IP address and device type):

- **YouTube** (Google). Reels and clips play in YouTube's official embedded player. To show tap-to-learn subtitles,
  Mimi asks YouTube for that video's captions while you watch. Google's privacy policy applies:
  https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **Jisho.org.** When you tap a word that isn't in Mimi's built-in dictionary, the word is looked up on Jisho.org.
  Only the word is sent. "Open in Jisho" links open Jisho.org in your browser.

Mimi doesn't send your saved words, progress or any personal details to these services.

## Permissions

- **Notifications**, only if you turn on the daily reminder. The reminder is scheduled on your phone; no push service is used.
- **Internet**, for the features above.
- **Vibration**, for light touch feedback.

## Children

Mimi is meant for teenagers and adults learning Japanese. It is not directed at children under 13 and doesn't
knowingly collect information from anyone.
